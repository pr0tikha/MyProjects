Privacy Policy for PayMind

Effective date: December 29, 2025

1. No Data Collection or Transmission

PayMind is designed with your privacy as our top priority. The application does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information, financial data, or anonymous identifiers to any external servers or third parties.

2. Local Data Storage

All data you enter into the PayMind app, including expense details, amounts, and due dates, is stored exclusively on your local device within the application's secure storage area (e.g., browser localStorage or IndexedDB). This data never leaves your device and is not accessible by us or any other party.

3. AI Feature

The "Suggest a time with AI" feature sends non-personal, generic data (payment category, due date) to a secure server to generate a suggestion. It does not send any personal identifiers, financial details, or user-specific information. The usage of this feature is optional.

4. Third-Party Services

PayMind does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

5. In-App Purchases and Ads

The app is completely free to use and does not contain any advertisements or in-app purchases.

6. Data Deletion

Because all data is stored locally, you have full control. You can delete individual expenses from within the app. Uninstalling the application from your device will permanently remove all of its associated data.

7. Contact Us
